Changes by moyu: select every raw ja record for Japanese; every zh/cmn record for
Chinese/Mandarin. Keep only each canonical page headword and its supplied POS,
readings/pronunciations, usage labels and Vietnamese gloss text; preserve Chinese
pronunciation variety labels. Canonical ruby supplies readings with unannotated
kana preserved. Do not expand inflections, form_of targets or script variants.
Examples, media, translations into other languages, etymology and link metadata
are omitted. Byte-identical duplicate records are deduplicated. JSON and gzip
packaging added. Missing readings/glosses remain empty; no AI or machine translation.

This is the complete relevant language subset of the pinned raw extraction,
not a promise of comprehensive Vietnamese vocabulary or error-free extraction.
Wiktionary and extractor limitations remain. In particular, some Chinese entries
contain character descriptions or romanization references rather than definitions.
