Changes by moyu: all published dictionary rows normalized into JSON. Traditional
and simplified spellings, source numbered pinyin, and complete slash-separated
glosses retained. No invented POS or readings. Entry ID is cedict: followed by the
SHA256 of the upstream row in UTF-8 without its newline. Gzip compression added.

The manifest records release, compressed size, SHA256 and entry count. Pinned
source metadata and rebuild instructions are in scripts/lexical/ of the source
distribution. Updates require manual source/license review, new pinned hashes,
normalizer tests and immutable versioned filenames. No automatic upstream lookup
or download occurs during dialogue analysis. No licence-imposed update schedule.
